Understand before you cut

Chunks that keep
their meaning.

Most pipelines flatten a file to text and split every N tokens — tables lose their headers, captions lose their figures, answers lose their questions. Cleave profiles the input first, maps the relationships inside it, and refuses the cuts that would break them.
